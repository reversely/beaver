How Beaver works

Beaver meets a newcomer in two places. Out in the city, the rover answers a question about what they see, asked from their phone. At the laptop, the desktop app answers their longer questions and keeps every exchange from both places as notebooks.

  • Rover takes the question from the newcomer's phone, looks through its camera, and answers aloud in their language.
  • Guard proxy runs on the rover and keeps their personal information from reaching the AI services.
  • Gemini writes the answer, and ElevenLabs speaks it.
  • Desktop app copies each rover turn every 60 seconds and files it into a notebook.

Guard proxy

A question about duties, documents, or deadlines can carry a phone number, an address, or a card number. The guard proxy keeps those on the rover.

  1. The rover keeps the recording in its own run folder.
  2. Whisper turns the recording into text on the rover.
  3. The guard proxy replaces personal information: "613 555 0142" leaves as "a phone number".
  4. Gemini receives the clean text and a photo.
  5. The guard proxy checks the answer before ElevenLabs speaks it.
